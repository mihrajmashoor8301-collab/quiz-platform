## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an interactive SaaS aesthetic engineered for high-velocity real-time quizzes, collaborative workshops, and executive keynotes. It merges the pulse-pounding kinetic engagement of consumer trivia platforms with the disciplined refinement, typographic clarity, and structural authority of tier-one enterprise products.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Electric & Focused:** Energetic without crossing into chaotic gamification. Transitions are swift, intent is legible, and states communicate instantly across massive presentation displays and personal handhelds alike.
- **Authoritative SaaS:** Clean, high-density dashboard layouts balance the vibrant real-time presentation engine. Presenters feel equipped with a precision tool, while participants experience frictionless, immediate interaction.
- **Tactile Competitiveness:** Answer targets evoke tangible, responsive physical buttons without resorting to cartoonish skeuomorphism.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Product leaders, enterprise facilitators, conference hosts, educators, and cross-functional teams participating in live assessments, pulses, and retrospectives.
- **Emotional Response:** Sharp anticipation, confidence in live technical stability, clarity under time pressure, and professional delight.

### Visual Style Direction
A sophisticated fusion of **Modern Corporate Architecture** and **Hyper-Vibrant Interactive Surfaces**. Key traits include:
- Pure slate and luminous white surface layering punctuated by high-potency Electric Indigo anchors.
- Delicate glassmorphic accents reserved strictly for elevated headers, floating status bars, and transient presentation HUD overlays.
- Restrained, tactile micro-elevations to retain instant physical affordance on mobile response screens.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated chromatic hierarchy designed to maintain strict boundaries between administrative utility, ambient presentation, and rapid competitive gameplay.

### Core Brand Accents
- **Primary (`#5842FF` - Electric Indigo):** The nucleus of interactive state changes, active selection outlines, primary calls to action, timer countdown rings, and participant connection badges.
- **Secondary (`#4329EB` - Twilight Violet):** The structural anchor for focused brand moments, presenter mode action headers, selected states under press, and high-emphasis hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Cyan Stream):** Supplementary indicator for live metadata, streak multipliers, participant reaction indicators, and secondary metrics.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Core):** Grounded slate spectrum powering borders, muted icons, tabular metadata, and secondary administrative text.

### Semantic Answer Palette
The quadruple answer palette is engineered for instantaneous peripheral recognition on participant viewports:
- **Card A / Option 1 (Coral Red):** `#EF4444` (Surface tint: `#FEF2F2`, Active border: `#DC2626`)
- **Card B / Option 2 (Cerulean Blue):** `#3B82F6` (Surface tint: `#EFF6FF`, Active border: `#2563EB`)
- **Card C / Option 3 (Amber Sun):** `#F59E0B` (Surface tint: `#FFFBEB`, Active border: `#D97706`)
- **Card D / Option 4 (Emerald Pulse):** `#10B981` (Surface tint: `#ECFDF5`, Active border: `#059669`)

### Surface & Border Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, low-strain cool slate backdrop providing maximum contrast against pure white cards.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density workspace tiles, presenter sidebar, and floating question matrices.
- **Border Crisp (`#E2E8F0`):** Default component delimiter ensuring crisp pixel boundaries across Retina and ultra-wide projection monitors.
- **Border Subtle (`#F1F5F9`):** Sub-dividers within nested analytics lists and live participant roster rows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is structured using **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, geometric headers, structural data labels, and gamified answer titles, partnered with **Inter** for dense tabular statistics, administrative settings, and narrative question bodies.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Display XL & Large Headlines:** Used for live arena projection modes, massive question intros, podium rollouts, and session countdowns. Numbers use tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate micro-jittering during live countdowns.
- **Headlines (MD & SM):** Employed for participant question prompts, workspace module groupings, modal headers, and live score cards.
- **Body Hierarchy (LG, MD, SM):** Standardized on Inter. Highly legible at small scales on mobile hand-held answer pads, ensuring instant comprehension under sub-second response pressures.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Plus Jakarta Sans at medium and bold weights, supporting tracking expansion (+0.04em on `label-sm`) for status indicators, player role badges, and leaderboard badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model establishes a dynamic responsive rhythm catering to two fundamentally distinct user surfaces: the **Presenter Studio / Enterprise Admin Dashboard** and the **Live Arena / Participant Mobile Pad**.

### Presentation & Studio Grid System
- **Desktop Dashboard / Stage (1200px and up):** 12-column fluid grid with `2rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` structural gutters. Studio view adheres to a 3-panel split: Live Stage / Slide Outline (240px fixed), Center Canvas (flexible 8-column equivalent), and Configuration/Realtime Telemetry Rail (320px fixed).
- **Tablet / Split View (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Presenter sidebars collapse into slide-over panels; live presentation matrices drop down to 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile Handheld Controller (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Buttons stretch to full device width with strict vertical tap targets exceeding 54px for non-glance interactions.

### Vertical Spacing & Component Rhythms
- Spacing follows a modular 4px/8px structural base.
- Gaps between interactive answer grids lock strictly to `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on widescreen projection formats.
- Canvas sections, scoreboard tiers, and metric groupings rely on `space-xl` to establish explicit separation without heavy horizontal divider rules.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers**, **Crisp Micro-Shadows**, and **Glassmorphic Presentation HUDs**.

### Depth Layers & Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base application canvas. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Paneling):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp `#E2E8F0` 1px border. Micro-shadow applied: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Answer Panels):** Raised answer cards and interactive option blocks. Enhanced tactile shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Active answers translate `2px` downwards on tap while shrinking the bottom shadow to 0px, simulating an authentic physical press.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating HUDs):** High elevation layer: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

### Glassmorphic Overlays
For live session controls, floating timer badges, and top-tier navigation bars:
- Surface fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`.
- Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(180%)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on balanced, modern curves (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem base) with deliberate pill-shaped excursions for live telemetry, scores, and status elements.

### Curvature Guidelines
- **Base UI Cards & Inputs (0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, settings containers, presentation slides, and question list rows use standard base roundedness.
- **Answer Selection Tiles (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Interactive quiz responses feature expanded 16px radius corners to soften the click target and prevent sharp terminal collision on mobile viewports.
- **Modal Containers & Arena Overlays (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Primary modal displays, live victory podium containers, and full-screen prompt cards.
- **Pill Shapes (Full Radius / 9999px):** Dedicated solely to meta tags, room pin codes, participant count pills, streak counters, and micro-tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Indigo (`#5842FF`) background with crisp white typography. Hover transitions to Twilight Violet (`#4329EB`). Active state drops 1px on Y-axis. Inner shadow `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, text Slate Core (`#0F172A`). Hover changes border to `#5842FF` with text tinting.
- **Destructive:** Border and text `#EF4444`, background transparent. Hover transitions to `#FEF2F2`.
- **Live Response Buttons (Participant Canvas):** Full-bleed semantic background tints (`Coral`, `Cerulean`, `Amber`, `Emerald`) with a saturated 2px bottom bevel (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 [Darkened Accent Hex]`). On active press, the button translates down 4px with zero shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (9999px), height 24px or 28px, uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Participant Pin Chip:** High-contrast slate card with monospaced letter spacing, holding a glowing green dot indicator for web-socket latency under 50ms.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** 44px standard height. White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius. Active focus switches border to `#5842FF` accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(88, 66, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px boxes, 4px border radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Checked state fills with `#5842FF` displaying an inset high-contrast white glyph.

### Cards & Question Matrices
- **Dashboard Metric Card:** White surface, subtle `#E2E8F0` border, internal padding `1.5rem`. KPI figures rendered in bold Plus Jakarta Sans with percentage sparkline badges positioned top-right.
- **Live Quiz Stage Display Card:** Presentation-mode viewport displaying the current prompt. Features full-bleed glassmorphic timer pill top-center, crisp question text in display typography, and balanced 2x2 grid slots for answer distributions.

### Specialized Components
- **Countdown Ring:** Radial SVG timer track in `#F1F5F9` with the active stroke transitioning dynamically from `#5842FF` down to `#EF4444` when under 5 seconds.
- **Live Leaderboard Row:** Horizontal card with podium rank container (Gold, Silver, Bronze badges), animated score differential tags, and dynamic real-time vertical translation animations.
